## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

The first version: a private AI writing assistant that runs on your own computer.

### Added
- Proofread, change the tone, reply, summarize, format, translate and write, in the window or over any app with a shortcut.
- Replies answer what the message says to you: thanks, wishes, news and questions.
- Models chosen for your computer: Fast (Gemma 4 E2B) with 8 GB of memory, Quality (Gemma 4 E4B) with 16 GB; switch them in Settings.
- Custom actions of your own, and a history that is off unless you turn it on, then encrypted.
