Notes

  1. Every preset has an icon, the same thin line icons as the rest of the app (Lucide), muted, in a fixed column so the names line up. The highlighted item's icon takes the accent. Tone: a briefcase for Professional, a cup for Casual, a hand with a heart for Polite, a smile for Friendly, a badge for Confident, arrows in for Concise and out for Elaborate, a megaphone for Social, a smile with a plus for Emojify. Format: a list, a table, an envelope.
  2. The Tone picker in Write and Full reply shows the chosen tone's icon too, from the same set, so a tone looks the same wherever it is picked.
  3. Languages get their own script, never a flag: a language is not a country (Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, Arabic are each spoken in many), and a script is what the reader recognises. On the right, the language's name in its own writing. The text's own language stays out of the list, as now, and the untested ones keep their "Best effort" heading.
  4. My actions are the user's own, so no fixed icon fits: each shows the first letter of its name on the accent's soft colour. New action keeps the plus, under a line.
