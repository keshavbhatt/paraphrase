Notes

  1. History sits in the window's header beside Settings and opens in place of the two panes; "Back to writing" returns with the text as it was. Ctrl+H opens it too. The popup has no history of its own: its runs land here.
  2. Search and filter: search looks through the text and the result of every entry (on this computer, in the decrypted database); the chips filter by action, one row of them, the other actions (Write, Summarize, Format, Translate, My actions) under More. Entries come newest first, grouped by day.
  3. One line per entry: the action with its preset or language, the app it came from (or none for the window), the time, and the start of the result. Arrow keys move through them; Enter copies the result.
  4. The entry: the text and the result as kept (for Proofread, the diff as in main-window.html). Copy result copies it; Use again puts the text back on the left with the same action and options, ready to run; the bin deletes this entry alone, asked on the page.
  5. Where it lives is always said at the bottom, with a way to the privacy settings (settings-privacy.html).
  6. Off: the History button still shows, and says plainly that nothing is kept, with a way to turn it on (which opens the privacy settings with the switch).
  7. Header buttons (approved 2026-10-27): History and Settings have 10px padding and sit 4px apart, so "On this device", History and Settings are evenly spaced (about 24px between words) when no button shows its background; the open or hovered one still shows its full background.
  8. A split (proposed 2026-10-27) between "Your text" and "Result": they share the height evenly, and dragging the split gives either more, for a long text or a long result. No line until the pointer is over it; each keeps at least 2 lines; the place is kept.
