Notes

  1. The working pill replaces the grey "· writing..." while a run is going: the accent colour on its soft background, a dot that pulses, the step in words and the seconds so far (DESIGN 4: slow is shown honestly, in words, with no bar that claims to know the end). It keeps the width of its longest text ("Checking, writing again"), so nothing in the header moves as the words change. When the run ends it gives way to the usual summary line (e).
  2. The shape of the answer: until the first word arrives, the result box shows soft lines with a slow shimmer: the shape of an email for Write and Full reply, a paragraph for Proofread, Tone and the other text actions, three cards for quick replies (d). The first word replaces them; while the words stream in, the caret at their end blinks (b). A user who asked the system for reduced motion gets the same pill and lines, without the pulse and shimmer.
  3. Stop is unchanged: the last button turns into Stop at the same width, Copy and Another version wait. The steps are only the ones the app knows for sure: "Reading your text" until the first word (this includes loading the model on a first run), "Writing" after it, "Checking, writing again" when the engine retries; quick replies say "Writing 3 replies" throughout.
